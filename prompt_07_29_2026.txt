alright, this is a nice first version, but there are several problems/needed changes to note:

1. the "hover a judge for details" docked tooltip should not show up or take space in the Change view of the tooltip.

2. the icon change was somewhat counterproductive. intentionally applying a blur makes the photos worse. i'd like the aliasing which occurs, perhaps as a result of downsizing or perhaps as a result of the way the image is being shown (i'm not sure what causes it), to be solved without causing other problems.

3. the hitbox for the draggable bar should be a bit wider, symmetrically. in addition to clicking and dragging the bar, when a part of the streamgraph view is clicked, it should snap the bar to that x-position.

4. the icons and numbers in the new Change view are too small, and overlap with the draggable bar too much (they should be moved more to the left, and numbers put on the left side of the icon instead of the right). the icons in the timeline interactive are also too small.

5. the stream polygons are too jagged. i believe some of this is avoidable when working with small counts, and some of it is also caused by the "more-current-at-center-and-older-at-periphery" arrangement. i'd like to flip this on its head and try that out.

6. currently, it looks like the Change view has a separate buffer box mechanic for D/R icons; the bumping of icons should occur regardless of party. furthermore, the icon location is not properly aligned with the stream.

7. in the Change view of the info pane, the Mark: < None | FedSoc | ACS > option should not show up.

8. there is currently a problem with the individual stream polygons. first, their on-hover black outlining is inconsistent, and appears to get disrupted by the adjacent polygons. furthermore, parts of the polygons which represent "0 judges of thickness during this time" should not be part of the geometry, as it is unnecessary complexity that we don't want an outline appearing for.

9. the timeline shown should scale (along the x-axis range) to exclude portions of time on the oldest/latest edge of the range, which consecutively have zero judges of thickness from any presidents.

10. there should be dashed lines in the background with plain-text president labels, like in the judicial appointments timeline, with the red/blue pastel background colors. no need for year labels for now.
