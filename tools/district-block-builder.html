District block builder

Builds the new district-seat block cluster feature (national view only): one shaped cluster of squares per circuit, sized to that circuit's total authorized district judgeships, hand-arranged to roughly resemble the shape of the circuit (and, assembled together in the Arrangement stage, the shape of the US). This tool covers the first two stages — Sampler (grid-fits a circuit's real boundary to a target block count), Editor (freehand tailoring of the resulting matrix), District linker (assign blocks to specific districts + red/blue/vacant colors + reading order), and Arrangement (position every circuit's linked cluster on one canvas, export for the map — not yet consumed by the live widget, see PROGRESS.md).
